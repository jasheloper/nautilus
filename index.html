Leasing vs. Financing Your Lincoln Nautilus

Undecided if you should lease or finance the 2026 Lincoln Nautilus? While there's no one-size-fits-all option, one of these paths will fit some drivers' lifestyles better than others. Below, we highlight just a few of the unique perks of leasing and financing.

For many, a Lincoln Nautilus for lease is ideal because it gives them the opportunity to upgrade to a luxury SUV with typically lower monthly payments than if they were to finance it. Leasing also provides the flexibility to get into a new ride more often, as most leases are 24 or 36 months long, allowing you to frequently enjoy the latest features and updates.

Keep in mind, however, that there are annual mileage restrictions, and you may have to pay for any damage or wear and tear incurred to the vehicle. When your 2026 Lincoln Nautilus lease is up, you can return the SUV and walk away, upgrade to a new Lincoln model through leasing or financing, or purchase it and continue driving it for as long as you want.

Although monthly payments for financing are often more expensive than monthly installments for leasing, you will be paying toward full ownership of the vehicle. As the owner, you won't have to worry about mileage restrictions or extra fees. Plus, with one of our available Lincoln Nautilus financing offers, taking home this SUV can be even easier. If you plan to drive your Lincoln Nautilus for many years, financing will likely be your best option.

2026 Lincoln Nautilus Power & MPG

A new Lincoln Nautilus lease or loan can feature one of two engines. The standard 2.0-liter turbocharged four-cylinder unit, which is mated with an eight-speed automatic transmission, impresses drivers with 250 horsepower and 280 pound-feet of torque.

The available 2.0-liter turbocharged four-cylinder hybrid engine, which is paired with a CVT, delivers 285 combined horsepower and 295 pound-feet of torque. It renders up to an EPA-estimated 29/31/30 MPG (City/Highway/Combined).1 Meanwhile, the gasoline engine produces an EPA-estimated 21/29/24 MPG (City/Highway/Combined).1 All 2026 Lincoln Nautilus variants come standard with AWD for streamlined driving dynamics.
